# Labelling guide (5 lines)

1. **cement**: binders sold by the bag or tonne: OPC/Portland, SRC, white cement, ready-mix bags, tile adhesive and grout that are cement-based.
2. **steel**: anything metal used for structure: rebar (any mm), mesh, binding wire, steel sections, anchor bolts.
3. **aggregates**: loose granular fill sold by m³ or tonne: sand (washed, dune, black), gravel, crushed stone, gabbro, road base.
4. **blocks**: pre-formed masonry units counted by the piece: concrete/hollow/solid/insulated blocks, interlock pavers, kerbstones, bricks.
5. **services**: anything that is not a material: delivery, transport, crane/pump hire, labour, testing, waste removal. Rule for ties: label by what is billed, not where it is used (e.g. "delivery of cement" = services). Arabic and English lines follow the same rules; normalise Arabic digits before labelling.

**Out of taxonomy:** invoice line 5, *Waterproofing membrane / رول عزل مائي*, fits none of the 5 classes. Don't force it. Mark it `unmapped` and send it for review (proposal: add a 6th class, `finishes / waterproofing`). It is kept out of training.
